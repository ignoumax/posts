IGNOU has officially started its admission process for the January 2026 session for Open & Distance Learning (ODL) programmes, *Last date is 31st March 2026*

IGNOU provides admissions in 2 sessions in a year, first is June session and second is January session, students can take admission in any of the two sessions.

# Procedure to take Admission in IGNOU

Students can register and get admission in IGNOU through *Admission Portals* for their programmes

Step by step process to complete the admission registration
1. Fill the form with required details like *Name*, *Father's Name*, *Mother's Name*, *Email*, *Mobile Number* & *Academic Details* like *Marks*, *Roll number* of Class 10th and Class 12th
2. Upload the asked and required documents in necessary format
3. Complete the whole process by paying the registeration fee
4. Download the Registration Reciept and save it for the later use

IGNOU will verify your data and once the verification is completed, You will recieve an Email stating their *Enrollment Number* (Most Important) and other details.
